## Script

I gave Claude two reels I liked, and it remixed them into this one. It's called Reel Remix, and it's completely free. It's not one skill either. It's five of them, and each one does a different job. The High Roller bets on the reels getting the most comments, the Detective studies every frame, the Brand Cop swaps their look for yours, and the Director writes the storyboard. But the craziest part? The Editor builds the whole reel in code. Cut for cut. Borrow the moves. Never the reel. To set it up, paste one link into Claude Code, and out comes yours. Comment REMIX and I'll send you the whole pack.
