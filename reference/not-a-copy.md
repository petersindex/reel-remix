# Borrow the moves, never the reel

A remix takes the **mechanics** of a reel and rebuilds them with the person's own material. These rules protect the person and the creators they learned from.

## Take

- Structure: the order of beats, where the hook lands, when the call to action arrives.
- Pacing: shot length, cuts per minute, holds.
- Devices: split screen, caption chips, a character acting out each line, a prop that carries the story, world-then-proof cards.
- Packaging style in the abstract: caption position, size, entrance type.

## Rebuild with the person's own

- Words: a new script about the person's topic. No sentence from the reference transcript appears in the remix.
- People: the person's face, footage, avatar or character. Never the reference creator's likeness.
- Look: colours, fonts and logo from `remix/brand.md`.
- Sound: the person's voice or a TTS voice; music the person owns or licenses.
- Media: no frames, screenshots, footage or audio from the reference in the final video.

## Check

Before a storyboard or render is handed over, compare its script with every reference `transcript.json`. Any run of six or more consecutive words in common gets rewritten.
